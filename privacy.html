Privacy Policy

Your PDFs never leave your phone.

Effective date: 7 November 2026 · Applies to the PdfToolKit Android app (package com.arunkaushik.pdftoolkit)

The short version
  • Every PDF feature — reading, merging, splitting, rotating, extracting and deleting pages — runs entirely on your device. Your files and their contents are never uploaded to us or anyone else.
  • There is no account and no sign-up. We never ask for your name, email or phone number.
  • The free plan shows ads from Google AdMob. AdMob receives standard device and usage information described below — never your documents.
  • Premium is bought through Google Play. We never see your payment details.

1. Who we are

PdfToolKit is developed by Arun Datt Kaushik, an independent developer based in India (“we”, “us”). For any privacy question, contact arun.idevs@gmail.com.

2. Your documents

PdfToolKit opens and edits PDF files only on your device. We do not operate any server that receives your files, their contents, their names or their metadata.

  • File access. The app uses Android’s system file and folder picker (Storage Access Framework). It can only see the files and folders you choose, and you can revoke that access at any time in Android settings or by choosing a different folder in the app.
  • Processed results. Merged, split or edited PDFs are created in the app’s private storage until you save or share them. Unsaved results are deleted automatically, and you can clear them in Settings → Storage.
  • Password-protected PDFs. When you open a protected PDF, your password is used only on the device to decrypt it. The password is not stored. A decrypted copy is kept in the app’s private storage only while the reader is open and is deleted when you close it.
  • Sharing. When you tap Share, the file is passed to the app you pick (for example email or a messaging app). What happens next is governed by that app.

3. Information stored on your device

To make the app work, the following is saved locally on your device only and is never sent to us:

  • Settings (theme, reader preferences, library folder)
  • Recent files, starred files, bookmarks and the last page you read
  • Your daily free-action count and cached Premium status

Uninstalling the app or clearing its data in Android settings removes all of this.

4. Advertising (free plan)

The free plan shows ads served by Google AdMob, a service of Google LLC. To show and measure ads and prevent fraud, the Google Mobile Ads SDK automatically collects and sends to Google:

  • Your choices. In the European Economic Area, the UK and other regions where it is required, the app asks for your consent before any ad is requested, and you can change it anytime in Settings → Ad privacy choices. Anywhere, you can reset or delete your Advertising ID in Android Settings → Privacy → Ads.
  • Premium users see no ads, and the app does not request ads for them.

5. Purchases

Premium subscriptions and the lifetime purchase are processed by Google Play. Google handles your payment details under its own privacy policy; we never receive your card or bank information. To check whether you have Premium, the app asks Google Play on your device for your purchases. We do not store your purchase history on any server.

6. What we do not do

  • No analytics or tracking SDKs added by us, and no crash-reporting service of our own.
  • No selling of personal data.
  • No access to your contacts, camera, microphone or precise location.

If you have opted in on your device, Google Play may share aggregated, anonymous crash and performance statistics with us through the Google Play Console. These do not include your files.

7. Children

PdfToolKit is not directed at children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

8. Permissions

  • Internet — used only for ads and Google Play purchases. Never used to send your files.
  • Notifications (optional, Android 13+) — to tell you when a long task has finished.

9. Your rights

Because we do not hold personal data about you on any server, there is nothing for us to access, export or delete on our side. Data stored on your device can be deleted by clearing the app’s data or uninstalling it. For data collected by Google for ads, see My Ad Center and the Google Privacy Policy. You can contact us with any request at arun.idevs@gmail.com.

10. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will update this page and its effective date. Significant changes will also be mentioned in the app’s release notes.
