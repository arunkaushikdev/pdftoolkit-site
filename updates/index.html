1.0.7 Build 8 · 14 November 2026, 11:50 AM IST

New features

  • NewA fresh Home screen — your recent PDFs, a Continue reading row and quick buttons to Open, Scan, Merge and Shrink, with a centre + button to open a PDF, scan with the camera or turn images into a PDF.
  • NewThe bottom bar hides while you scroll and comes back when you scroll up, so lists get the whole screen.
  • NewEdit PDF, redesigned — a full-height page with floating Text, Cover and Highlight tools, page count and zoom. Pinch to zoom in for exact placement.
  • NewChange text that is already in a PDF — tap a printed line, edit it right on the page (tap a letter to put the cursor there), and the new text matches the original size, style and colour. Resize the box with its side handle.
  • NewImage to text — take a photo or pick an image and PdfToolKit reads the text on your phone (English and Hindi). Fix any mistakes, then copy or share it. Find it in Tools and under the + button.
  • NewScanned PDFs — press and hold to select text even in pages with no text layer, including Hindi, using on-device text recognition.
  • NewText selection now has a floating Copy, Highlight and Select all bar, and a magnifier while you drag the handles.
  • NewPinch and double-tap zoom in single-page view.
  • NewAdd to Starred from the Reader's three-dots menu.
  • ImprovedTools are grouped, and ads sit at the top of each tab instead of between your files.
  • ImprovedSelecting text is much faster — no more waiting on "Reading text…".

Fixes

  • FixedThe separate "Copy text" button is gone — press and hold on the page selects text where you touch, and you copy from the bar.
  • FixedDouble-tap and pinch zoom no longer jerk, and the page no longer flashes white for a moment after zooming.
  • FixedThe magnifier stays visible when you select text at the top of a page.
1.0.6 Build 7 · 10 November 2026, 9:10 PM IST

New features

  • NewSelect text in the Reader — press and hold a word, drag the handles to extend, then Copy or Highlight.
  • NewHighlights — mark text in yellow, then save them into the same PDF or as a new copy. If you leave with unsaved highlights, PdfToolKit asks whether to save or discard them.
  • NewFree-shape photo crop — move each corner on its own and the area is flattened into a straight page, like a document scanner.
  • NewRotate photos by 90° and straighten tilted ones with a slider before they become a PDF.
  • NewCheck for updates and What's new in Settings.
  • ImprovedThe Recent list on Home updates instantly — a PDF you save or open appears at the top right away.
  • ImprovedRenaming a PDF from the Reader works for files from your phone's storage: Android asks for permission, or a renamed copy is saved where you choose.

Fixes

  • FixedCropping a photo taken with the camera now shows the picture properly and no longer closes the app, even with very high-resolution photos.
  • FixedPhotos taken with the camera, or cropped, now show their preview in the selected files list.

Known issues

Last updated 14 November 2026, 1:20 PM IST · Latest release 1.0.8

  • Saving highlights into the original PDF. On Android 10 and older, Android does not let an app change a PDF it only opened from the Recent or Library lists. Use Save as a new copy — it always works, and the new copy appears at the top of Recent. PDFs opened with Open PDF can usually be updated in place.
    Open Seen in 1.0.6 · First listed 10 Nov 2026 · Last update 10 Nov 2026, 8:34 PM IST
  • Renaming a PDF in place. For the same reason, some files can't be renamed on Android 10 and older. PdfToolKit saves a renamed copy in a folder you choose instead; the original stays untouched.
    Open Seen in 1.0.6 · First listed 10 Nov 2026 · Last update 10 Nov 2026, 8:34 PM IST
  • Brief flicker when zooming. Double-tap or pinch zoom in the reader can show a very short flicker or a thin grey band (the gap between pages) for a few milliseconds. It does not affect reading, the page content or your file, and we're still working on making it perfectly smooth.
    Open Seen in 1.0.6 · First listed 14 Nov 2026 · Last update 14 Nov 2026, 9:52 AM IST
