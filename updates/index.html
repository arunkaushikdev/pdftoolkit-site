Every PdfToolKit release, in plain language. Currently rolling out through closed testing on Google Play.

Known issues

  • Saving highlights into the original PDF. On Android 10 and older, Android does not let an app change a PDF it only opened from the Recent or Library lists. Use Save as a new copy — it always works, and the new copy appears at the top of Recent. PDFs opened with Open PDF can usually be updated in place.
  • Renaming a PDF in place. For the same reason, some files can't be renamed on Android 10 and older. PdfToolKit saves a renamed copy in a folder you choose instead; the original stays untouched.
  • Selecting text in scanned PDFs. Scanned or photo-based PDFs have no text to select yet. Text recognition (OCR) is planned for a later release.

New features

  • NewSelect text in the Reader — press and hold a word, drag the handles to extend, then Copy or Highlight.
  • NewHighlights — mark text in yellow, then save them into the same PDF or as a new copy. If you leave with unsaved highlights, PdfToolKit asks whether to save or discard them.
  • NewFree-shape photo crop — move each corner on its own and the area is flattened into a straight page, like a document scanner.
  • NewRotate photos by 90° and straighten tilted ones with a slider before they become a PDF.
  • NewCheck for updates and What's new in Settings.
  • ImprovedThe Recent list on Home updates instantly — a PDF you save or open appears at the top right away.
  • ImprovedRenaming a PDF from the Reader works for files from your phone's storage: Android asks for permission, or a renamed copy is saved where you choose.

Fixes

  • FixedCropping a photo taken with the camera now shows the picture properly and no longer closes the app, even with very high-resolution photos.
  • FixedPhotos taken with the camera, or cropped, now show their preview in the selected files list.
  • FixedA PDF that you edited no longer shows its old pages until the app is restarted.
