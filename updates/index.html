What's new

Every PdfToolKit release, in plain language. Currently rolling out through closed testing on Google Play.

1.0.4 Build 5 · 10 November 2026 Latest
  • NewCompress PDF — choose Light, Medium or High and see the estimated file size for each before you start. Light is free; Medium and High are Premium.
1.0.3 Build 4 · 9 November 2026
  • NewEdit PDF — add text anywhere on a page. Tap to place it, pick a size, and it's saved as part of the PDF.
  • NewEdit or delete any text box you've added, before saving.
1.0.2 Build 3 · 8 November 2026
  • NewImages to PDF — scan photos with your camera or pick them from your gallery, turned straight into one PDF, one photo per page.
  • NewPhotos taken sideways are automatically straightened before they're added to the PDF.
  • ImprovedSettings now links directly to this Privacy Policy and the Terms of Service.
1.0.1 Build 2 · 7 November 2026
  • NewFirst closed-testing release.
  • FixedPassword-protected PDFs — like e-Aadhaar — now open right in the Reader with their password.
  • FixedFile and folder rename no longer shows an error after succeeding.
  • FixedThe bookmark icon now renders correctly.
  • ImprovedPremium pricing always matches Google Play exactly — nothing is ever over- or under-quoted.
